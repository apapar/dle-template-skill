# Website Statistics

*DLE documentation topic #56*

The output of this section is configured in the **stats.tpl** file. This section configures the display of overall website statistics.

`{datenbank}` | Total database size
---|---
`{news_num}` | Total number of news items
`{news_allow}` | Published news items
`{news_main}` | Published on the main page
`{news_moder}` | Awaiting moderation
`{comm_num}` | Number of comments
`{user_num}` | Registered users
`{user_banned}` | Banned users
`{topusers}` | List of the best authors on the website
`{news_day}` | News items published per day
`{news_week}` | News items published per week
`{news_month}` | News items published per month
`{comm_day}` | Comments published per day
`{comm_week}` | Comments published per week
`{comm_month}` | Comments published per month
`{user_day}` | Users registered per day
`{user_week}` | Users registered per week
`{user_month}` | Users registered per month
