# Adding News

*DLE documentation topic #17*

You can access the admin center at http://website.com/admin.php. After authorization, you will be able to add, edit, and delete news, manage users and templates, configure the script, manage images, and much more.

When adding a news item to the website, you need to specify the article title. The **SEF URL** field of the article is optional and is used when SEF URLs are enabled. You can enter your own text there, which will appear in the browser address bar, or leave it empty, in which case the text will be generated from the article title according to the transliteration rules. The **Date** field sets the date of the news item; if the **Current date** checkbox is selected, the current date will be inserted automatically with time correction applied, or you can clear this checkbox and enter the date manually using the convenient calendar by clicking the icon next to the field. If you specify a date that has not yet arrived, the news item will be published automatically when that date is reached, provided the news item is approved for publication. You can also choose the **category** to which the article belongs. After that, use the convenient buttons for inserting tags to write your news item in the short and full description fields; if additional fields have been created, enter the information there as well. **Attention:** the **Full description** field is not required; if it is empty, the text will be taken automatically from the **Short description**. Using the **[Upload image]** link, you can insert images uploaded to the server into the news text or upload your own image directly for this article. It is also possible to upload an image directly from another server without first downloading it to your own computer. **Attention:** if you upload your own images for this article, they will be deleted from the server automatically when the news item is deleted.
